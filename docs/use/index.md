# Use Keera

This part is for developers who use models through Keera Gateway.

These pages use the public instance, `https://gateway.keera.ch`. If your
company runs its own gateway, use its address instead.

1. [Sign in](sign-in.md) to the panel and to the `keera` CLI.
2. [Connect your tools](connect.md): Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode or any
   OpenAI client.
3. Check [My access](my-access.md) to see your keys, your budget and the
   models you may use.

The [Playground](playground.md) lets you try a model in the browser, and
[The API](api.md) explains what your tools can call. To just chat, use
[Keera Chat](chat.md).
